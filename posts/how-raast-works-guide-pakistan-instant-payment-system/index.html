How Raast Works: A Practical Guide to Pakistan's Instant Payment System

What a Raast ID is, how to create one with your mobile number, what transfers cost, and how merchant QR payments fit in.

Fatima Noor Fintech & Regulatory Analyst
• 13 Oct 2026
24/7 INSTANT SETTLEMENT

Raast is the instant payment system built and operated by the State Bank of Pakistan. It connects banks, microfinance banks and electronic money institutions on a single real-time rail, so money sent from one account reaches another within seconds, at any hour, on any day.

The system was rolled out in phases starting in 2021: bulk payments came first, followed by person-to-person transfers, and later merchant payments through QR codes and request-to-pay. Each phase was designed to move everyday transactions away from cash.

For most users the key concept is the Raast ID. Instead of sharing a long IBAN, you link your mobile number to one of your bank accounts, and anyone can send you money by entering that number in their own banking app.

Creating and Managing a Raast ID

Open your bank or wallet app, find the Raast section and register your mobile number as your Raast ID. A mobile number can be linked to only one account at a time, so if you want payments to land in a different bank you must first unlink it from the old one and then register it in the new app.

Costs, Limits and Safety

Person-to-person Raast transfers for individuals are free of charge under State Bank directions. Daily and per-transaction limits are set by each bank, so check your app before moving large amounts. Always confirm the recipient name shown on the confirmation screen before approving, because instant payments cannot be pulled back once sent.

Raast for Shops and Small Businesses

Merchants can accept Raast payments through a static or dynamic QR code issued by their bank. Funds settle straight into the merchant account rather than sitting with a payment aggregator, which helps small shops with cash flow.

Executive Takeaways & Key Insights

  • Raast is the State Bank of Pakistan's real-time payment rail, available round the clock.
  • A Raast ID links your mobile number to one bank account so people can pay you without an IBAN.
  • Person-to-person transfers are free for individuals; limits vary by bank.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I link one mobile number to two bank accounts on Raast?

No. A Raast ID can point to only one account at a time. Unlink it in the first bank's app before registering it with another.

Is Raast the same as IBFT?

No. IBFT is the older interbank transfer service run through 1LINK, while Raast is the State Bank's own instant payment system with a mobile-number alias.
