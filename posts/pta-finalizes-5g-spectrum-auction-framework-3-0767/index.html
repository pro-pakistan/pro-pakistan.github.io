PTA Finalizes 5G Spectrum Auction Framework: 300 MHz in 3.5 GHz Mid-Band Earmarked for Commercial Launch

Pakistan Telecommunication Authority prepares nationwide spectrum auction, providing cellular mobile operators with flexible installment payments and ten-year tax incentives.

Zeeshan Ahmed Principal Telecom & Cloud Analyst
• 10 Oct 2026
300 MHZ MID-BAND SPECTRUM

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) has concluded statutory stakeholder consultations on the 5G Spectrum Auction Framework, allocating 300 megahertz of prime mid-band spectrum in the 3.5 GHz (C-band) alongside low-band 700 MHz frequencies for commercial rollout.

Designed to overcome commercial operator capital expenditure hesitations, the finalized policy allows cellular mobile operators to pay spectrum license fees in phased installments over five years, linked to strict infrastructure coverage benchmarks across urban centers and industrial corridors.

Telecom sector leaders emphasize that the deployment of mid-band 5G will address acute mobile data congestion across metropolitan hubs while enabling low-latency private networks for industrial automation, seaport logistics, and telemedicine clusters.

Mid-Band C-Band and 700 MHz Coverage Pairing

The regulatory blueprint pairs 3.5 GHz high-capacity spectrum for dense urban centers with 700 MHz coverage spectrum for deep indoor building penetration and rural highway connectivity, optimizing network capital expenditure.

Fiberization Benchmarks and Tower Infrastructure

PTA has mandated that licensed operators achieve minimum 40% tower fiberization milestones within eighteen months of spectrum allocation, driving major investments into dark fiber rollouts.

  • Installment payment terms and fiberization mandates balance operator CAPEX with rapid rollout.
  • Initial commercial coverage prioritized across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and economic zones.
