PTCL Group and Special Technology Zones Authority Sign $120M Agreement to Deploy Sovereign Hyperscale AI Compute Cloud in Islamabad

Public-private consortium secures dedicated tier-IV green data center infrastructure with 48 petaflops of FP8 tensor compute for domestic enterprises and government workflows.

Zeeshan Ahmed Principal Telecom & Cloud Analyst
• 11 Oct 2026
48 PFLOPS SOVEREIGN COMPUTE

Pakistan Telecommunication Company Limited (PTCL Group) and the Special Technology Zones Authority (STZA) have finalized a landmark $120 million investment agreement to inaugurate the nation's premier sovereign hyperscale AI compute facility in the Islamabad Special Tech Zone.

The greenfield data center, powered entirely by a dedicated 25-megawatt solar and hydroelectric hybrid microgrid, features liquid-cooled GPU clusters designed to deliver over 48 petaflops of specialized tensor acceleration for domestic researchers, banking institutions, and software exporters.

By ensuring full data residency within national borders, the sovereign infrastructure addresses critical compliance mandates set by the Ministry of Information Technology while insulating domestic artificial intelligence startups from soaring foreign-exchange cloud billing.

Liquid-Cooled Sovereign Architecture

The facility implements direct-to-chip liquid cooling loops to achieve an industry-leading Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) rating of 1.14. High-bandwidth optical interconnects link the primary compute pods directly with Pakistan's submarine cable landing stations in Karachi.

Enterprise and Academic Compute Subsidies

Under the STZA charter, accredited academic research institutions and certified Pakistani software exporters will receive subsidized compute vouchers, enabling local engineering teams to pre-train and fine-tune specialized multilingual language models without offshore hardware dependencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the new sovereign AI cloud data center situated?

The facility is strategically located within the Islamabad Special Technology Zone, supported by dedicated redundant fiber backbones and clean microgrid power.

How will local Pakistani software developers access the infrastructure?

Accredited startups and universities can request API access and subsidized compute vouchers directly through the STZA Innovation Portal.
