Solar Net-Metering Overhaul: SBP Gross-Metering Framework & Battery Storage Incentives

Ministry of Energy details updated export buyback tariffs alongside 0% customs duty on commercial lithium energy storage systems to stabilize the national grid.

Fatima Noor Senior Markets & Energy Editor
• 07 Oct 2026
3.2 GW ROOFTOP SOLAR INSTALLED CAPACITY

Pakistan's private rooftop solar generation has surged past 3,200 megawatts, creating both immense household power relief and unprecedented daytime voltage fluctuations on the national distribution grid. In response, the Ministry of Energy and NEPRA have rolled out a revamped Net-Billing framework designed to incentivize residential and commercial Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

Under the updated regulatory structure, existing residential net-metering connections remain grandfathered under their original bilateral terms, dispelling rumors of retroactive penal tariffs. However, new commercial installations exceeding 15 kW are provided lucrative peak-hour feed-in tariffs if equipped with certified lithium iron phosphate (LFP) storage, enabling them to discharge saved solar power into the evening peak demand window (6:00 PM to 10:00 PM).

Complementing this grid stabilization drive, the Federal Board of Revenue has announced zero customs duties and sales tax exemptions on imported industrial grade battery packs and hybrid bi-directional inverters. This incentive has already prompted major textile mills and commercial logistics centers in Faisalabad and Karachi to deploy multi-megawatt battery farms, cutting reliance on high-cost imported LNG peaker plants.

Grandfathering Clause for Existing Residential Net-Meter Customers

NEPRA confirmed that residential consumers who obtained net-metering three-phase bi-directional meters prior to the new regulation retain their 1:1 units offset rights, safeguarding investments made by ordinary middle-class homeowners.

Time-of-Use Export Premiums for Evening Battery Discharge

Commercial solar installations that feed stored power into the national grid during high-demand night hours receive an enhanced premium rate of PKR 28 per unit, transforming solar farms into dispatchable baseload assets.

Zero Duty on Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) Energy Cells

Customs exemptions for LFP battery packs have driven localized retail battery pack prices down by 26%, making 10 kWh home battery backups economically viable with sub-3-year payback periods.

  • 0% customs duty on LFP battery systems accelerates shift toward decentralized clean storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my existing home net-metering connection be canceled or reduced?

No. Official NEPRA directives confirm existing residential net-metering licenses will remain honored for the complete duration of their 3-year renewable contracts.

What is the lifespan of modern LFP batteries being installed in Pakistan?

Modern Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery packs boast 6,000+ deep discharge cycles, translating to 12 to 15 years of daily operational service with minimal capacity degradation.
