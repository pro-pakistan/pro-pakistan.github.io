PTA 5G C-Band Auction Blueprint: 300 MHz Spectrum Rollout Across Major Metros

Pakistan Telecommunication Authority finalizes base pricing, fiber-to-the-tower obligations, and 3.5 GHz mid-band allocations for nationwide gigabit mobile data.

Zeeshan Ahmed Telecom & Infrastructure Bureau Chief
• 07 Oct 2026
300 MHz AUCTIONED SPECTRUM POOL

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) has released the final information memorandum for the upcoming 5G commercial spectrum auction, earmarking 300 MHz of prime mid-band spectrum across 3.5 GHz (C-Band) and 2.6 GHz alongside millimetric wave frequencies. The framework establishes a strategic 10-year installment payment plan denominated in PKR to shield telecom operators from currency volatility.

Under the mandatory rollout obligations, participating cellular mobile operators (CMOs) must activate commercial 5G coverage across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and Peshawar within six months of spectrum award, expanding to all provincial divisional headquarters within 24 months. Furthermore, operators must guarantee minimum downlink speeds of 100 Mbps at cell boundaries.

To resolve the chronic fiberization bottleneck where fewer than 15% of mobile cell sites were previously connected to optical fiber backhauls, the government has waived right-of-way (RoW) fees along national highways and inter-city railway corridors. This fiber-to-the-tower mandate is poised to unlock mobile gigabit broadband for over 45 million smartphones nationwide.

C-Band 3.5 GHz Allocation and Currency De-Risking Structure

To prevent telecom operators from overextending balance sheets during foreign exchange fluctuations, spectrum payments are indexed with local rupee installments rather than upfront USD lump sums, ensuring financial sustainability for network capex.

Aggressive Metro Rollout Milestones and Quality-of-Service Mandates

Operators must achieve 100 Mbps minimum median throughput at cell edges and 99.9% uptime. Industrial special economic zones and university campuses receive priority coverage mandates.

Right-of-Way Reforms & National Fiberization Surge

By standardizing right-of-way approvals across municipal bodies, telecom infrastructure providers are accelerating optical fiber laying, increasing fiberized cell towers from 15% to an estimated 42% by late 2027.

Executive Takeaways & Key Insights

  • 300 MHz of high-capacity 5G spectrum slated for auction with domestic currency installment protections.
  • Initial commercial launch covers Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and provincial capitals within 180 days.
  • Zero-fee Right-of-Way reforms accelerate fiber-to-the-tower backhaul connectivity across Pakistan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will consumers need new SIM cards for 5G in Pakistan?

No, existing 4G LTE USIM cards will automatically connect to 5G Standalone and Non-Standalone networks once 5G-compatible devices enter coverage areas.

What speeds can Pakistani mobile users expect on 5G?

Real-world commercial tests on 3.5 GHz C-Band deliver typical download speeds ranging from 450 Mbps to 1.1 Gbps, with latency under 12 milliseconds.
